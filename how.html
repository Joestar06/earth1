A NEW CATEGORY

A camera keeps footage.
Earthory keeps events.

Where are the keys? What did the doctor just say? What was actually decided in the meeting? What happened before the accident? A recording cannot answer any of these. Reality happens once; memory should not get only one chance too.

ONE · HUB · MEMORY OS

Four layers,
one chain.

Hardware reaches into reality; software turns reality into value. Four layers each handle one stretch, with no break in between, and you only touch the last one.

01

Earthory One｜reality sensing

Time, place, people and surroundings are captured together. The device first decides locally whether a moment is worth keeping, and the vast majority of uneventful time is never stored.

02

Earthory Hub｜local processing

The data boundary for homes and fixed spaces. Magnetic charging, local caching, encrypted uplink. Sensitive recognition and judgement finish at this layer, and raw material does not leave it by default.

03

Memory OS｜long-term understanding

Fragments are assembled into an event, and the event is placed on a long-term timeline. The same person, place or object appearing at different times is linked automatically, forming a searchable event structure.

04

User value｜retrieve · remind · understand

One sentence in plain language brings the moment back. The answer comes with its source, which you can open and check yourself. Throughout, you hold four controls: the physical switch, local-first processing, permissions and deletion.

PROTECT · RECALL · REMEMBER

Protect the present. Retrieve the past. Remember a lifetime.

One system doing one thing at each of three timescales. These are not three features, but three ways of using the same memory at different moments.

PROTECT · the present

You cannot have both
watching every change.

Something is off at home, a promise is coming due, you left without something — it speaks up when it actually matters, briefly, precisely, and in a way you can act on.

RECALL · retrieve the past

The things that matter,
get buried by time.

Time, stress and impression rewrite memory together. What comes back is a time, a place, the context and the evidence — not a vague “sometime that summer”.

REMEMBER · a lifetime

The most ordinary days
disappear the fastest.

The everyday moments that seemed unremarkable and can never be returned to are kept as long-term memory you can choose, take with you and pass on.

Recording keeps data from vanishing, understanding gives it meaning, and action lets it change outcomes.

THE SENSING LAYER

It works in the background,
so you do not have to think about it.

Earthory One can be worn, or placed at home, on a desk, or in a car. You never take out a phone, and never decide to start recording. It is simply present, and always shows you whether it is working.

A status light, a visible recording indicator and a pause you can press at any time are the three highest priorities in the hardware design.

PERSONAL ST-VDB · WORLD MODEL

Five entry points,
a map that is yours alone.

People, places, objects, events and moments are five entry points into the same memory. Time, space, entities, relationships, sources and permissions are organised together, so you can reach a journey through a person, or an afternoon through an object.

This map belongs only to you. It is never merged with anyone else's memory, never used to train a model, and never sold. What it grows into is an understanding of your particular life context, not a generic profile.
